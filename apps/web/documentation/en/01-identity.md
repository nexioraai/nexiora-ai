# What is Deribfy?

> Deribfy is an AI website and online store builder. You describe your business in plain language, and Deribfy generates a complete, working website. A single subscription lets you create a showcase website, an online store, or a dropshipping store.

**Scope:** all site types.

**Questions this page answers:** What is Deribfy? What is Deribfy for? Who is Deribfy for? Is Deribfy a website builder? How many products does Deribfy offer? Do I need to code to use Deribfy?

## One product, one subscription

Deribfy is not a product range. It is **one product** with **one subscription**. What you choose when creating a site is its **type**, not a different plan.

The site type is chosen at creation and **cannot be changed afterwards**. Changing type means creating a new site.

## The three site types

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Showcase website** | Presents your business: what you do, your services, your story, how to reach you. No selling. |
| **Online store** | A showcase website, plus selling **your own products**, whose stock you manage. |
| **Dropshipping store** | A store whose products come from a **supplied catalogue**. You hold no stock. |

Deribfy capabilities are cumulative. Every online store has everything a showcase website has. Every dropshipping store has everything an online store has.

## Who it is for

Small businesses, independent professionals, artisans, retailers, and people selling online for the first time. Deribfy suits those who know their business but have no technical background, and who want a site that is **online and findable**, not merely built.

No programming, design or hosting knowledge is required.

## Problems Deribfy solves

- Writing and designing a site when you are neither a copywriter nor a designer.
- Putting a site online without configuring hosting.
- Preparing a site for search engines and AI answer engines without technical knowledge.
- Selling online without building a store.
- Selling without holding stock, when you choose dropshipping.

## What Deribfy is not

- **Not a marketplace.** Your store is yours, not a listing on a shared platform.
- **Not a block-by-block builder.** The site is written for your business, not assembled from an empty template.
- **Not a hosting provider to configure.** Hosting is included and managed.
- **Not an SEO agency**, and **not a guarantee of Google ranking**.
- **Not an accounting tool.**

## Limits

- The site type is fixed at creation.
- A site is not publicly accessible before the subscription is active.
- If the subscription stops, the site is taken offline.
