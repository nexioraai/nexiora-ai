# Frequently asked questions

> Short answers to the most common questions about Deribfy. Each answer links to the page that develops it.

**Scope:** all site types.

## About Deribfy

**What is Deribfy?**
Deribfy is an AI website and online store builder. You describe your business in plain language, and Deribfy generates a complete, working website. A single subscription lets you create a showcase website, an online store, or a dropshipping store. → *What is Deribfy?*

**Do I need to code?**
No. No programming, design or hosting knowledge is required. → *What is Deribfy?*

**What kinds of sites can I create?**
Three: a showcase website, an online store, or a dropshipping store. The type is chosen at creation and cannot be changed afterwards. → *Site types and capabilities*

**How many languages?**
36, including Arabic with right-to-left script. Your site is generated in the language you used. → *Generation and editing*

## Creating and publishing

**How do I create a site with Deribfy?**
You describe your business in plain language. Deribfy generates the site, you edit it, preview it, then publish it. An account is required from the start. → *How it works*

**Can Deribfy create my site automatically?**
Yes. Copy, structure, images, colours and layout are generated from your description. You then change whatever you want. → *Generation and editing*

**Is my site visible before publication?**
No. Before the subscription is active it is private. The preview is the only way to see it, and it is restricted to you. → *How it works*

**How do I publish my site?**
You press **Publish**. That activates your subscription; as soon as payment is confirmed, your site is online. → *How it works*

**Can I edit my site after publishing?**
Yes. After publication, your site is not frozen. Your changes appear online immediately. There is no re-publish step. → *How it works*

**Do I need to re-publish after a change?**
No. There is no re-publish step. → *How it works*

**What happens if I stop my subscription?**
The site is taken offline. Its content is not deleted. → *Limits*

## Selling

**Can I create an online store?**
Yes. You sell your own products, with product pages, cart, card payments, orders, shipping and discount codes. → *Online store*

**Does Deribfy do dropshipping?**
Yes. You choose products from a supplied catalogue, set your margin, approve them, and orders are handed off to fulfilment automatically. You hold no stock. → *Dropshipping*

**Who handles dropshipping orders?**
They are recorded in your dashboard and then handed off to fulfilment automatically. You handle neither stock nor parcels. → *Dropshipping*

**What products can I sell?**
Your own stocked products; resold finished products from a catalogue; print-on-demand products carrying your design; or print-on-demand products your customer personalises before buying. → *Dropshipping*

**What is the difference between an online store and dropshipping?**
An online store sells products you own and ship. A dropshipping store uses a supplied catalogue and shipping is automated. → *Online store*

## Domains

**Can I buy a domain through Deribfy?**
Yes. You search for a name, reserve it, and pay an annual subscription. Registration, DNS configuration and the Google steps are handled for you. → *Domains*

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes. Deribfy shows you two DNS records to add at your provider, then verifies the connection. You keep your domain with your provider. → *Domains*

**Does Deribfy configure DNS?**
For a domain bought through Deribfy, yes, entirely. For a domain you bring, you add the two records shown to you. → *Domains*

**Is my site served over HTTPS?**
Yes. The certificate is issued automatically once DNS has propagated and the domain is verified. This can take from a few minutes to a few hours. → *Domains*

## Visibility

**Does Deribfy help with SEO?**
Yes, technically: sitemap, robots file, structured data, `llms.txt` file, clean URLs, and for a custom domain, verification with Google and sitemap submission. → *SEO, Google and AI visibility*

**Does Deribfy submit my sitemap to Google?**
Yes, for sites using a custom domain, automatically, with retries on failure. → *SEO, Google and AI visibility*

**Does Deribfy guarantee search rankings?**
No. Deribfy prepares and automates the technical work. Google alone decides on indexing and ranking. No service can promise a position. → *SEO, Google and AI visibility*

**What is AI visibility?**
Deribfy queries AI answer engines about your brand, derives a score out of 100, keeps the history, and suggests actions. → *SEO, Google and AI visibility*

## Content

**Does Deribfy generate images?**
Yes. Deribfy generates visuals for your marketing content from a description. → *Marketing and content*

**Does Deribfy offer a blog?**
No. Customer sites do not include a blog. → *Limits*
