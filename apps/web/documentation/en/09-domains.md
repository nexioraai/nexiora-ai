# Domains

> Every published site is online immediately at a Deribfy address. A custom domain is an additional, optional step, available once the site is published. Two paths exist: buy a domain through Deribfy, or connect a domain you already own.

**Scope:** all site types.

**Questions this page answers:** Can I buy a domain through Deribfy? Can I use my own domain? Does Deribfy configure DNS? Is my site served over HTTPS? How long does setting up a domain take? Can I change domain? Can I detach my domain? What happens to my domain if I archive my site?

## Two paths

| | Buy through Deribfy | Connect your own |
|---|---|---|
| Who owns the domain | bought for you | you already own it |
| DNS configuration | **handled for you** | **you add two records** |
| Google verification | automatic | automatic |
| Renewal | annual, through Deribfy | with your provider |

## Buy a domain through Deribfy

1. You **search** for the name you want.
2. Deribfy checks its **availability** and shows its **price**.
3. You **reserve** it. Payment is an **annual** subscription.
4. Deribfy handles everything: registering the domain, configuring DNS, connecting it to your site, and the verification steps with Google.

**There is nothing for you to configure.**

Some domain extensions cannot be registered automatically. Deribfy tells you **before any payment**.

## Connect your own domain

If you already own a domain, connect it. You keep it with your current provider: **Deribfy needs no access to your account**.

Deribfy shows you the **two DNS records to add** — one for the root address, one for the `www` address — with their exact values. If your domain requires an extra ownership check, the matching record is shown too.

You add those records at your domain provider.

## Verification and propagation

Connection status is displayed: pending, being configured, verified, or a problem. A **Check now** button requests an immediate re-check, so you are never left without an answer.

DNS changes take **from a few minutes to a few hours** to propagate, depending on your provider.

## HTTPS

Your custom domain is served over **HTTPS**. The certificate is issued automatically by the hosting infrastructure once DNS has propagated and the domain is verified; this step can take **from a few minutes to a few hours**.

Both the root address and the `www` address work: whichever you did not set as your main address redirects to the other, keeping the page and its parameters.

## Managing a domain afterwards

- **Change domain** — connect a different one at any time.
- **Detach** — removes the link between a domain and a site. The site stays online at its Deribfy address. **Detaching never cancels a domain.**
- **Stop renewal** (purchased domains) — the domain stays active until its expiry date; it is not deleted. You can restore renewal before it expires.
- **Archive a site** — detaches its domain but cancels nothing. A purchased domain keeps its own renewal cycle, and Deribfy tells you so at the time.

## Ownership of a purchased domain

A domain bought through Deribfy is registered by Deribfy with its registration partner. **Deribfy commits to transferring control to you on request**, within applicable rules and the timeframes imposed by the relevant bodies, and keeps no rights over the domain beyond the service period.

## Limits

- A custom domain is only available once the site is published.
- DNS propagation and certificate issuance times depend on your domain provider.
- Some extensions cannot be registered automatically.
- An already expired domain cannot be recovered.
