# Marketing and content

> Deribfy generates marketing content for your business: copy and visuals, produced from a short brief and consistent with what your site already says about your products, tone and audience.

**Scope:** all site types.

**Questions this page answers:** Does Deribfy generate marketing content? Does Deribfy generate images? Can I generate visuals for social media? Does Deribfy offer a blog?

## What is generated

- **Marketing copy**, tailored to your business and audience.
- **Visuals**, generated from a description.

Deribfy already knows your business, your products and your site's tone: generated content builds on that rather than starting from nothing.

## How it works

You briefly state what you want to communicate. Deribfy produces the matching content. You review it, adjust it if needed, and decide how to use it.

## Limits

- **Deribfy does not post to social media for you.** Generated content is handed to you; distributing it is up to you.
- **Customer sites do not include a blog.** Deribfy does not generate blog articles for your site.
- Generated content is a proposal: you remain responsible for what you publish.
