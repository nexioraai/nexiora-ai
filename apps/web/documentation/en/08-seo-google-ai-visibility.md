# SEO, Google and AI visibility

> Every site published with Deribfy is generated with the technical elements search engines and AI systems expect: sitemap, robots file, structured data, `llms.txt` file and stable URLs. For a custom domain, Deribfy also automates ownership verification with Google and sitemap submission. **Deribfy prepares and automates the technical work; Google alone decides on indexing and ranking.**

**Scope:** all site types.

**Questions this page answers:** Does Deribfy help with SEO? Does Deribfy generate a sitemap? Does Deribfy configure Google? Does Deribfy submit my sitemap to Google? Does Deribfy guarantee search rankings? Will my site be on the first page of Google? What is AI visibility?

## What is prepared automatically

With no action from you, every published site has:

- an **XML sitemap**, kept in step with your published pages and products;
- a **robots file** pointing search engines to that sitemap;
- **structured data** describing your products and their prices, your frequently asked questions, your postal address and geographic coordinates;
- an **`llms.txt` file**: a plain-text summary of your business, sections, products and frequently asked questions, designed to be read by AI systems;
- **clean, stable URLs**, with a dedicated page per product.

## What is automated for a custom domain

When your site uses a custom domain, Deribfy performs the steps needed for Google to discover it:

1. placing the **ownership verification record**;
2. **verifying ownership** with Google;
3. **registering the site** in Google's tracking tool;
4. **submitting the sitemap**.

These steps run automatically and are **retried on failure**. You need no Google account and no manual setup.

## What Deribfy does not promise

These elements make a site **technically ready** to be found and understood. They guarantee neither a position, nor an indexing date, nor traffic.

**Indexing and ranking are Google's decisions, made on its own criteria and its own schedule. No service can promise a position in search results. Deribfy cannot either.**

## AI visibility

Deribfy measures how visible your brand is to AI answer engines.

- Deribfy queries those engines about your business.
- It derives a **score out of 100**.
- It keeps the **history**, so you can see the trend.
- It lists **concrete actions** to improve that score.

As with search, the score measures a situation; it does not guarantee improving it.

## Limits

- Google decides on indexing and ranking.
- Technical preparation replaces neither content, nor reputation, nor time.
- Verification with Google applies to sites using a custom domain.
