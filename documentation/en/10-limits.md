# Deribfy limits

> This page gathers what Deribfy does not do, or does only under certain conditions. It exists so that nobody — human or automated system — credits Deribfy with a capability it does not have.

**Scope:** all site types.

**Questions this page answers:** What does Deribfy not do? Can I change my site type? Is my site visible before publication? What happens if I stop my subscription? Does Deribfy offer a blog? Does Deribfy guarantee search rankings?

## Product limits

- **The site type is fixed at creation** and cannot be changed afterwards. Changing type means creating a new site.
- **A site is not publicly accessible before the subscription is active.** The private preview is the only way to see it beforehand.
- **If the subscription stops, the site is taken offline.** Its content is not deleted.
- **Customer sites do not include a blog.**
- **Deribfy does not post to social media for you.**

## Store limits

- On an online store, stock, packing and shipping remain your responsibility.
- In dropshipping, the available catalogue depends on the way of being supplied chosen at creation, and that cannot be changed afterwards.
- Delivery times and destinations in dropshipping depend on supply.
- A dropshipping store exposes fewer product-editing tools than an online store.

## Domain limits

- A custom domain is only available once the site is published.
- Some extensions cannot be registered automatically; Deribfy says so before any payment.
- DNS propagation and HTTPS certificate issuance times depend on your domain provider.
- An already expired domain cannot be recovered.

## Visibility limits

- **Google decides on indexing and ranking.** Deribfy prepares and automates the technical work; no service can promise a position in search results.
- The AI visibility score measures a situation; it does not guarantee improving it.
- Technical preparation replaces neither content, nor reputation, nor time.

## What Deribfy is not

- Not a marketplace.
- Not a block-by-block builder.
- Not a hosting provider to configure yourself.
- Not an SEO agency.
- Not an accounting tool.
