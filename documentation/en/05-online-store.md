# Online store

> A Deribfy online store sells **your own products**, which you create and whose stock you manage. It includes everything a showcase website has, plus product pages, cart, card payments, order management, shipping and discount codes.

**Scope:** online store and dropshipping store. **Not available** for a showcase website.

**Questions this page answers:** Can I create an online store with Deribfy? What products can I sell? How do I add a product? Does Deribfy handle payments? How are orders managed? Can I create discount codes? What is the difference with dropshipping?

## What you sell

Your own products. You create them, set their prices, track their stock, and ship them.

## What you control for each product

- the **name**;
- the **description**;
- the **price** and **currency**;
- the **images**;
- the **stock quantity**;
- whether it is **published**;
- whether it is **available for sale**;
- its **position** in the list.

An unpublished product, or one withdrawn from sale, is not shown to your visitors and cannot be bought.

## The buying journey

Your visitors browse a **dedicated page per product**, add to the **cart**, then pay **by card**. The order then arrives in your dashboard.

## Managing your sales

- **Orders**: track their status from your dashboard.
- **Shipping**: configure your shipping rules.
- **Discount codes**: create and deactivate them whenever you want.

## Online store or dropshipping?

| | Online store | Dropshipping store |
|---|---|---|
| Where products come from | you create them | supplied catalogue |
| Stock | you hold it | you hold none |
| Shipping | you handle it | handed off automatically |
| Price | you set it | you set a margin |

The choice is made **when the site is created** and cannot be changed afterwards.

## Limits

- A store is only publicly accessible after the subscription is active.
- Stock, packing and shipping of your own products remain your responsibility.
- The site type cannot be changed after creation.
