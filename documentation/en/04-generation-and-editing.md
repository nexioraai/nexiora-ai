# Generation and editing

> Deribfy generates a complete site from a plain-language description, then lets you change it in two ways: by talking to an assistant, or by editing each element directly. Both remain available before and after publication.

**Scope:** all site types.

**Questions this page answers:** What exactly does Deribfy generate? What language is my site written in? How do I edit my site? Can I ask for changes in plain language? Can I change colours and theme? Does Deribfy generate images?

## What Deribfy generates

From your description, Deribfy produces:

- your business **name** and **tagline**;
- the **sector** it belongs to;
- the **headline and subheadline**;
- an **about** section;
- **content sections** with their items;
- **testimonials**;
- your **contact details**, when you provided them;
- a **colour palette**;
- **images** illustrating the site.

The site is not a template with replaced text: it is written for your business from what you described.

## Language

Your site is generated **in the language you used** to describe your business. 36 languages are supported, including Arabic with right-to-left script.

## Editing by conversation

You ask for a change in plain language, for example:

- "change the main colour to dark green";
- "rewrite the about section, shorter and more direct";
- "add a section about our opening hours";
- "change this product's price to 39";
- "remove this testimonial".

The assistant **proposes** the change and **you approve it**. Nothing is applied without your agreement.

## Editing directly

Panels let you change things by hand: text, colours, theme, sections, contact details, and — depending on the site type — products, orders and payments.

The four visual themes are interchangeable at any time, without losing your content.

## Available tools depend on the site type

A showcase website and an online store have the widest set of content and product tools. A dropshipping store exposes fewer, because its catalogue comes from a partner rather than from you.

## Before and after publication

Editing works the same way before and after going live. After publication, your site is not frozen. Your changes appear online immediately. There is no re-publish step.

## Limits

- The assistant proposes changes; it never publishes or charges anything on its own.
- Available editing tools depend on the site type.
